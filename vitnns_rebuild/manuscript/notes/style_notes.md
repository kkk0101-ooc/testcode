# Style notes from the local related papers

The draft adopts structural habits from the supplied papers without copying their wording.

1. **Dou et al. (2023):** introduce one visual object, define its construction before experiments, and pair schematic figures with real network outputs. The draft follows this with the learning-image definition, operator-specific derivation, and real ViT chain.
2. **Li et al. (2023):** organize a complex transformer analysis around explicit questions at the head, patch, and layer levels. The draft similarly separates response origin, intervention fidelity, explainer comparison, and depth allocation.
3. **Komorowski et al. (2023):** place quantitative explanation evaluation ahead of qualitative interpretation and distinguish class-targeted from class-agnostic methods. The draft reports registered contrasts before discussing visual atlases.
4. **Yeh et al. (2023):** move from a global transformer question to a concrete representation and then to application scenarios. The draft uses the same narrowing rhythm in the Introduction.

Sentence-level style is deliberately more compact than several source articles: direct subject-verb structure, measured quantities in result sentences, and one evidence boundary per claim.
