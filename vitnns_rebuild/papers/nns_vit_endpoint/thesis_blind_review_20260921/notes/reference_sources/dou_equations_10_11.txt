Equation (10) states that the layer-to-layer contribution coefficient is the weight multiplied by the destination neuron's piecewise-linear activation gate:
    c_pq^(l-1,l) = S(x_q^l) w_pq^l.

Equation (11) writes the input-to-target contribution matrix with the terminal weight row outside the product over earlier layer contribution matrices:
    C_q^(0,l) = W_q^l product_{i=1}^{l-1} C^(i-1,i).

Thesis interpretation: a pre-activation target excludes its terminal activation gate; a post-activation target includes it. This check binds equation identities and target semantics, not software-level parity with unavailable original-author code.
