Scope authorized by the user's eight-point feedback on 2026-09-20: expand the paper figures, co-locate manuscript and images, rewrite formal prose using local article examples, replace sparse validation plots with tables, improve visible examples, add concept/decomposition figures, layerwise learning-image/feature-map pairs, and actual method comparisons in blue-background high-intensity style.

Reference articles: Understanding neural network through neuron level visualization (Dou et al., 2023), especially Figures 1–5, 13–14; How Does Attention Work in Vision Transformers? A Visual Analytics Attempt. We adopt explanatory progression and visual organization, not their claims or images as our results.

Figure 19 uses evaluation orders 4 (mantis), 14 (sloth bear), and 15 (little blue heron). Following user rejection of those cases for Figure 20, ViT-B examples were reselected from the other 13 pre-retained images by the presealed Endpoint/input-detail readability rule in `figure20_reselection_protocol.md`. The selected orders are 1 (baboon), 13 (stethoscope), and 7 (offshore platform). No comparison-method output was read before selection. These remain presentation-selected illustrations, not population evidence. Quantitative conclusions retain the complete prior cohorts.

New computation is limited to six image-model illustrations on the existing two frozen checkpoints, using the original image preprocessing and GT logit. CPU execution checks source image/checkpoint hashes. For layerwise pages, each block selects the channel with maximum spatial variance of patch activation, using only forward activations; its scalar is half the sum of squared patch values. Its actual native 14×14 feature map accompanies its 224×224 Endpoint. Channels can differ across layers; their identities are recorded. Additional channel-zero depth maps use patch-sum targets.

Method comparison uses the same image/model/GT logit for Endpoint and Grad-CAM. Grad-CAM uses the final block's norm1 output, patch-token mean gradient channel weights, weighted activation sum and ReLU. Raw attention is the final block's head-mean CLS-to-patch weights. Rollout averages heads, adds identity, row-normalizes and multiplies the 12 attention matrices in forward order. Attention and rollout are class-agnostic. Endpoint patch-max display takes max of positive signed RGB-sum Endpoint in each native 16×16 patch and bilinearly upsamples; this is a display variant, not a new attribution method. No ordinary CNN CAM is claimed for a CLS-readout ViT.

Main learning images use positive signed RGB-sum Endpoint in a fixed deep-blue/cyan/yellow/red palette, scaled by positive P99 with maximum fallback, gamma=0.5 for learning images and absolute views; gamma=1 for forward features and comparison region scores. No smoothing is used for pixel Endpoint. All nonzero signs, absolute mass and complementary signed/absolute views remain in local supplementary pages and raw arrays. Positive maps describe coordinate-scaling terms, not finite causal support. Original images appear on every real-map page. Display scales are recorded and shape comparisons do not imply equal magnitude.

Formal scientific conclusions remain the original 11/12 component family association, unsupported all-family omnibus and spatial-utility criterion, and the registered spatial/depth analyses. New display computations do not alter those decisions or introduce population tests.

Numerical review before final rendering: CPU-vs-archived-GPU float32 relative L2 differences reached 2.96e-4 for ViT mantis; float64 reduced it to 7.73e-5. ViT bear float64 differed from the archived GPU result by 4.27e-4. These are recorded backend/precision diagnostics, not a change to the scientific records. The new display implementation is checked independently against the unmodified native CPU graph at relative L2 < 1e-7. Initial arrays are retained under evidence/initial_display_attempt. Spatial variance replaces total activation energy for channel selection to avoid selecting nearly spatially constant activation offsets; this choice uses forward values only.

Additional mantis forward maps were computed for comparison with the bear before final rendering. The complete layerwise main pages retain the bear so that concept, structure and depth views share one recognizable input; the mantis and heron are retained in method comparisons.
