## Paper-level evidence chain

The manuscript follows the Stage M chain:

1. **Representation:** original-NNS fidelity, registered scalar targets, and input-coordinate maps.
2. **Contribution:** attention Q/K/V and routing/value cuts, residual identity/branch cuts, closure,
   JVP, and mechanism trajectories.
3. **Intervention:** M1 component replacement, M2 spatial replacement, and M5 reference analysis.

The chain is cumulative but not substitutive: a later intervention result calibrates the use of a
representation; it does not rewrite the mathematical identity or closure result.
