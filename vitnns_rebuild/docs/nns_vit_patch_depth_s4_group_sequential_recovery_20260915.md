# S4 group-sequential recovery amendment

Status: `RESOURCE_DRIVEN_EFFECT_BLIND_BUT_PARTIAL_VALIDITY_STATUS_KNOWN_GROUP_SEQUENTIAL_RECOVERY`. This is not the original blind preregistration. It is frozen after some S4-R4 typed terminal states, including a small number of `NA_MATCHED_CONTROL_UNAVAILABLE`, were visible, but before any formal P1/P2/D1 cohort aggregate, direction, mean, confidence interval, p-value, Holm result, gatekeeping result, or scientific decision was read or computed. The relaxed valid/NA/matching gates are disclosed as post-terminal-status design changes. The scientific effect thresholds, directions, endpoint definitions, and strong FWER ceiling remain unchanged.

The fixed class order is the S2-R4 hash order `SHA256(NNS_VIT_PATCH_DEPTH_S2_R4_FORMAL_CLASS_COHORT_V1 + NUL + annotation_wnid)`, ascending digest then WNID. Looks are the strict class-rank prefixes 0–199, 0–383, 0–511, 0–639, and 0–871, with exactly two frozen confirmatory images per class. Future tranches are closed until the preceding CPU-only interim returns `CONTINUE_TO_NEXT_LOOK`. Existing validated S4-R4 immutable terminals may be rebound regardless of look, but their numeric outcomes remain sealed until their look opens.

The cumulative familywise spending targets 0.025, 0.040, 0.045, 0.048, and 0.050 are not ordinary p-value thresholds. Each of the seven nonempty P1/P2/D1 intersections receives a local sequential test. At an intersection of size m, the look-specific incremental spend is split across its components: P1/P2 receive incremental alpha divided by m and each D1 direction receives incremental alpha divided by 2m. The controlling boundaries are the exact standard-normal quantiles of those incremental allocations. A union bound over looks and components controls every local intersection at its cumulative spending target under arbitrary endpoint dependence; closed testing requires every containing intersection to reject, proving strong FWER at most 0.05. Canonical nested-look boundaries are retained as a noncontrolling sensitivity reference. Monte Carlo with 1,000,000 replicates per configuration/scenario validates implementation but is not the proof.

STOP_SUCCESS requires complete topology, no unexplained implementation/data failures, endpoint valid fraction at least 0.85, NA fraction at most 0.15, P2 matching at least 0.85, ordinary 95% CI half-width at most 0.015, all three elementary hypotheses rejected by closed sequential testing with repeated-look adjusted p-values and confidence intervals consistent with the fixed substantial-effect thresholds, and pre-seal design eligibility. STOP_SUCCESS prevents every later tranche claim. If L5 is valid but the first layer is not fully supported, S4 closes with `FIRST_LAYER_GATE_OPEN=false`, D2 remains not tested, and only primary-results sealing is authorized.
