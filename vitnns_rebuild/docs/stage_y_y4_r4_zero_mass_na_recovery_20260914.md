# Stage Y4-R4 prospective missing-value and validation recovery

Y4-R3 prospectively bound spatial support and patch mixing to each block's
all-head aggregate attention branch, but stopped on the first clean source
because block 11 has zero patch-token matrix mass and nonzero CLS mass. That
sealed R3 outcome does not authorize a new component choice. Y4-R4 therefore
keeps the R3 cut mapping unchanged and fixes only registered missing-value and
validation behavior. R3 and every earlier root remain immutable.

Y4-R4 changes only missing-value propagation. A zero-mass `T_b` produces NA for
T-dependent patch-mixing descriptors; valid CLS-routing and joint cancellation
are still reported. An image-level contrast requiring any NA block remains NA,
is not filled or replaced, and is excluded only from that endpoint's paired n.
If the registered minimum is not met, that hypothesis and its claim are
`INVALID`; other claims and workflow validity remain independent. Sign-flip and
Holm keep INVALID hypotheses in family accounting with a non-rejecting effective
p of 1 while reporting raw p and estimate/CI as null.

The three Y2 completed-budget records whose sealed arrays are VALID but whose
intervention payload was never produced remain valid for array-derived
endpoints. Their A1/B6/B7 coordinates are retained as NA. No intervention is
reconstructed or rerun. Full intervention AUC/control rows, all registered
top-k/bottom-k curves, all branch component summaries, all block/head tables,
matrix-entry estimates, middle-depth contrasts, perturbation condition-depth
summaries, correctness strata and available raw-attention associations are
emitted as descriptive or confirmatory tables according to Y0.

Producer and independent-validator source reads, CPU/wall/I/O usage and
operation counts are recorded separately and then reconciled into final access
and resource ledgers. The independent pass recomputes sources, primitives,
aggregates, every bootstrap/sign-flip replicate, Holm results, claim decisions,
tables, sidecars and inventories without trusting producer summaries.

All source identities, the sealed R3 cut mapping, metrics, thresholds, directions, RNG rules,
replicate counts, samples, conditions and truth tables remain unchanged. The run
is CPU-only, performs no empirical work and does not start Y5.
