`STAGE_X_X2_R2_COVERAGE_GATED_RECOVERY_20260909`. Authorization:
`X2_R2_COVERAGE_GATED_NUMERICAL_RECOVERY_ONLY`.

This prospective amendment is sealed before any new X2-R2 model load, JPEG
decode, prediction, attribution, ROAD, stability, or method-difference outcome.

`NO_GO_CLASS_LOGIT_COMPLETENESS`, with 197 valid clean routes, one actually
executed failed route, and two never-started routes.

nonconvergence or execution failure is tolerated only when all 200 expected
routes have a real attempted or source-validated prior-attempt terminal, at
least 190/200 routes are VALID overall, and every model has at least 38/40
VALID routes. FAILED, NA, not-started, and interrupted remain distinct. No
sample may be removed, replaced, imputed, retried until favorable, or relabelled
VALID.

and full-model IG identity on every VALID route, Holm membership, atomic
publication, data isolation, and implementation sealing remain zero-tolerance

native-unfused execution path, NNS-v2 mathematics, 135 complete cuts, schedule
