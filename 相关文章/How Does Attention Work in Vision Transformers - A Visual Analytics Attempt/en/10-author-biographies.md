# Author biographies

**Yiran Li** is a Ph.D. candidate in computer science at the University of California, Davis. She received her B.S. degree in Mathematical Sciences from Zhejiang University in 2018. Her research interests are visual analytics and interpretable machine learning.

**Junpeng Wang** is a research scientist at Visa Research. He received his B.E. degree in software engineering from Nankai University, M.S. degree in computer science from Virginia Tech, and Ph.D. degree in computer science from the Ohio State University. His research interests are broadly in visualization, visual analytics, and explainable AI.

**Xin Dai** is a staff research scientist at Visa Research. He received his B.E. and M.S. in computer science from Beijing Jiaotong University in 2012 and 2016, and Ph.D. in computer science from Worcester Polytechnic Institute in 2022. His research interests are in data mining and machine learning.

**Liang Wang** is a principal research scientist at Visa Research. His research interests are in data mining, machine learning, and fraud analytics. He received his Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Faculte Polytechnique de Mons, and his B.S. degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation and M.S. degree in Systems Engineering from Tianjin University. Before joining Visa, he worked at Yahoo, eBay/PayPal, and FICO on bankcard fraud detection. He is the inventor of more than 20 patents and has published more than 30 papers.

**Chin-Chia Michael Yeh** is a staff research scientist at Visa Research. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of California, Riverside. His dissertation, “Toward a Near Universal Time Series Data Mining Tool: Introducing the Matrix Profile,” received a Doctoral Dissertation Award Honorable Mention at KDD 2019. His research interests are in data mining, machine learning, and time-series analysis.

**Yan Zheng** is a senior staff research scientist at Visa Research. She received her Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Utah in 2017. Her research interests are in data mining, machine learning, and representation learning.

**Wei Zhang** is a principal research scientist and research manager at Visa Research. His interests include big-data modeling and advanced machine-learning technologies for the payment industry. He previously worked at Facebook, Nuance Communications, and IBM Research. He received his bachelor’s and master’s degrees from the Department of Computer Science at Tsinghua University.

**Kwan-Liu Ma** is a distinguished professor of computer science at the University of California, Davis. His research lies at the intersection of data visualization, computer graphics, human-computer interaction, and high-performance computing. He was elected IEEE Fellow in 2012, received the IEEE VGTC Visualization Technical Achievement Award in 2013, and was inducted into the IEEE Visualization Academy in 2019.
